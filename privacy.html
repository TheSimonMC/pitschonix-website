Privacy by design

Privacy.

Pitschonix is a free community network. We only use the data that is genuinely needed to operate the game, protect the network and save your settings.

Download as PDF
In short

No sales. No tracking. No paywalls.

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or hidden analytics.
  • There are no paywalls and no purchasable gameplay advantages.
  • We do not share data with third parties for their own purposes.
01 / Scope

When this notice applies

This notice applies to the Minecraft server, the website pitschonix.eu and their connected network features. By using the server or website, you confirm that you have read and accepted this notice. If a voluntary feature requires separate consent, we will ask for it separately.

02 / Necessary data

What is processed technically

To keep the network working and fair, we may process the following data in particular:

  • Minecraft username and UUID;
  • IP address and technical connection data in time-limited server and security logs;
  • game progress, coins, statistics, ranks, clan, friend and party features;
  • selected language, cosmetics and other personal network settings;
  • moderation and security data where needed to investigate a rule violation.
03 / Purpose & sharing

Why the data is used

Processing is limited to operating the network, saving your progress, preventing abuse, diagnosing errors and protecting network security. We do not share data for advertising, sales or profiling.

Technical hosting and infrastructure providers may process data only where required to operate the systems securely. They may not use it for their own advertising.

04 / Website

A deliberately simple website

The Pitschonix website currently uses no advertising or analytics trackers, sells nothing and requires no user account. External links open the relevant platform, where that provider’s privacy rules apply.

05 / Retention & rights

As little and as briefly as possible

Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting, security or abuse prevention. Persistent gameplay features remain stored for as long as the player profile is needed by the network.

You can request access, correction or deletion through the official Pitschonix Discord. Security, evidence or ban data may be retained longer where a legitimate protective reason requires it.
